How it works

Step into the field. Every decision you make, from the first question to the final verdict, shapes how well you contain the outbreak.

  1. Get authorization. Report to the District Health Officer before entering the field.
  2. Investigate. Interview doctors, health workers, patients and community leaders. Keep the village's trust.
  3. Sample and test. Collect the right samples and request PCR, serology, culture, biochemistry and more.
  4. Give your verdict. Name the pathogen, source and route, back it with evidence and propose containment.
