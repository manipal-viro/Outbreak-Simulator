How it works

1. Get authorization
Report to the District Health Officer before entering the field.
2. Investigate
Talk to doctors, health workers, patients and community leaders. Keep the village's trust.
3. Sample and test
Collect the right samples, request PCR, ELISA, biochemistry and more.
4. Give your verdict
Tell the DHO the pathogen, the source, the tests you ran and your containment plan.
